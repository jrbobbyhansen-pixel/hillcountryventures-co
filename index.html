How I Build

Philosophy

"Build businesses that run without me so I can spend time on faith, family, and the outdoors."

Every venture here is built to a single standard: does it reduce or remove a dependency on my time? If a tool, system, or partnership doesn't move toward autonomy, it doesn't ship. I'd rather have one business that runs itself than five that need me every day.

I work with partners who bring what I don't have — domain expertise, sales teams, operational capacity. My role is product, systems, and the connective tissue between ideas and execution.
